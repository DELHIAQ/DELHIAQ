Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · App: DELHIAQ

This Privacy Policy describes how DELHIAQ (“we,” “us,” or “the app”) handles information when you use our iOS application. We aim to collect only what is necessary to provide air-quality information for the Delhi National Capital Region (Delhi NCR).

1. Overview

DELHIAQ displays air quality indicators such as US AQI, PM2.5, and PM10, along with forecasts and historical trends for Delhi NCR monitoring zones. Environmental data is obtained from third-party open APIs. We do not require you to create an account to use the app.

2. Information We Collect

We do not sell personal information. We do not integrate third-party advertising or analytics SDKs for tracking.

  • On-device preferences: Settings you choose in the app—such as default monitoring zone, auto-refresh interval, and favorite station—are stored locally on your device (e.g., UserDefaults within the app sandbox). This data is not uploaded to servers operated by us.
  • Optional location: If you grant “While Using the App” location permission, the map feature may show your approximate position on the map for orientation only. Location is used on-device for display purposes. Air-quality API requests use fixed coordinates for Delhi NCR zones; providing your personal location is not required for those requests.
  • Network activity: The app connects to the internet to retrieve air-quality and forecast data. Requests may include technical parameters such as latitude, longitude, and time range for selected zones. They do not include your name, email address, or account identifiers because the app has no login system.

3. Third-Party Services

Air-quality data is provided by Open-Meteo (including CAMS-related sources). Your device communicates directly with Open-Meteo when you refresh data, view forecasts, or open history. Open-Meteo has its own terms and privacy practices; please review their website. Use of their data is subject to their attribution requirements (e.g., CC BY 4.0).

Apple MapKit may be used to display station locations. Processing by Apple is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

4. How We Use Information

  • To show current, forecast, and historical air-quality information;
  • To remember your preferences and improve your experience;
  • To display your location on the map when you have granted permission.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may disclose information only:

  • As needed to operate features through service providers such as Open-Meteo and Apple (see Section 3);
  • When required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the public.

6. Retention and Security

Preferences remain on your device until you delete the app or clear its data. Air-quality responses may be cached briefly on your device (for example, to avoid repeated network calls within a short window). DELHIAQ does not maintain a central user database or backend that stores your identity.

No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure. Please keep your device protected.

7. Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us using the developer contact on the App Store product page.

8. Your Choices

You can revoke location permission in iOS Settings, adjust in-app preferences, or uninstall the app to remove locally stored data.

9. International Users

DELHIAQ focuses on Delhi NCR. If you use the app from outside India, your device may still send API requests to Open-Meteo servers according to their infrastructure and policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the effective date at the top when we do. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. Material changes may be noted in release notes where appropriate.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at DELHIAQ@outlook.com.
